# ShiftFlow — store listing (English)

## App name (≤ 30 chars)
ShiftFlow: Shifts & Income

## Short description (≤ 80 chars)
Shift calendar and income tracker. Offline, no account, no ads.

## Full description (≤ 4000 chars)

ShiftFlow is a beautiful, simple way to track work shifts — made for everyone on a schedule: baristas and waiters, couriers, warehouse staff, nurses, retail workers, security guards, tutors and freelancers.

Mark a shift on the calendar and ShiftFlow works out what you earned for the day, the month and the year.

HIGHLIGHTS
• Shift calendar: a shift or a day off in one tap, swipe for the next month
• Up to 10 workplaces, each with its own color, rate and currency
• Hourly or fixed pay per shift
• Several shifts in one day — side jobs, split shifts
• Pay adjustments: tips, bonuses, deductions
• Notes for any day
• Pattern schedules: 2/2, 3/3, 5/2 or your own — to the end of the week or month
• Totals for any range: select dates and instantly see pay, shifts and hours
• Finance: month total, each workplace's share, monthly statistics and your best month of the year
• Home screen widgets: Today, a Month calendar that grows to the whole month, and Week with shift names — in your theme colors
• Export to Excel (.xlsx) and CSV for any range, per workplace or all at once
• Import shifts from Excel, CSV or a backup — merged without duplicates

PRIVACY
• No sign-up, no account
• Works fully offline
• No ads, analytics or trackers
• Your data stays on your device
• Back up to a file and restore whenever you like

DESIGN
• Dark and light theme, or follow the system
• 12 accent colors and 4 paper types: plain, blueprint, white, kraft
• Adjustable text size, corner style and calendar labels
• Battery-saver mode for low-end phones
• English and Russian

ShiftFlow — your schedule, your income.

## What's new (3.4.0)
Several shifts in one day — side jobs or split shifts, each with its own hours, adjustment and pay. The day page lets you add another shift, replace a workplace or remove a shift, with a day total at the bottom. Calendar cells show up to two stamps, and pattern schedules add a second job's shifts to days that are already marked. The “Timesheet” look — paper, ink and stamps — stays.
